Privacy Policy

Internet Cable N TV is operated by Zaz International Inc, an independent reseller. We care about your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What We Collect

Information you give us: When you fill out a form or call us, we may collect your name, phone number, email, service address, ZIP code, and anything else you choose to share.

Information we collect automatically: When you visit the site, we gather basic technical details like your IP address, browser type, device, and the pages you view.

Call recording: Calls with us may be recorded for quality and training. By calling us, you agree to this.

Cookies & tracking: We use cookies and analytics tools to understand how the site is used and improve it. You can turn cookies off in your browser anytime.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to show available internet, fiber, wireless, and TV options; connect your request to the right provider; contact you about services you asked about; improve the site; and meet legal requirements.

3. How We Share It

Zaz International Inc is an independent reseller and does not provide services directly. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information with leading providers and carriers, analytics tools, authorized partners when needed to handle your request, and legal authorities if required by law.

4. Calls & Texts

By giving us your number, you agree we and our partners may contact you by call, text, or automated technology about available plans. Consent is not required to buy anything. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out anytime by replying STOP or contacting us.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information only as long as needed to help you, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. After that, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete your data, unsubscribe from marketing, or opt out of sale/sharing of your data by emailing info@internetcablentv.com.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Security

We use reasonable protections such as SSL encryption. No online method is 100% secure.

9. Other Websites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

10. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time and will post changes here.
