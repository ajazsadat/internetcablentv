Privacy Policy

Internet Cable N TV is operated by Zaz International Inc, an independent reseller. We care about your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. What We Collect

Information you give us: When you fill out a form or call us, we may collect your name, phone number, email, service address, ZIP code, and anything else you choose to share.

Information we collect automatically: When you visit the site, we gather basic technical details like your IP address, browser type, device, and the pages you view.

Call recording: Calls with us may be recorded for quality and training. By calling us, you agree to this.

Cookies & tracking: We use cookies, Google Analytics, and Google Tag Manager to understand how the site is used and improve it. You can turn cookies off in your browser anytime, though some features may not work as well.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to:

  • Show you available internet, fiber, wireless, and TV options for your area
  • Connect your request to the right provider
  • Contact you about the services you asked about
  • Improve the site and our service
  • Meet legal requirements

3. How We Share It

Zaz International Inc is an independent reseller and does not provide services directly. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share it with:

  • Leading providers and carriers, to match you with plans and process your request
  • Analytics tools like Google Analytics, to measure site performance
  • Authorized partners, only when needed to handle your request
  • Legal authorities, if the law requires it

4. Calls & Texts

By giving us your number, you agree we and our partners may contact you by call, text, or automated technology about available plans. Consent is not required to buy anything. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out anytime by replying STOP or contacting us.

5. How Long We Keep It

We keep your information only as long as we need it to help you, meet legal obligations, and resolve any disputes. After that, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

6. Your Choices

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Unsubscribe from marketing anytime
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your data
  • Email info@internetcablentv.com to make a request

7. Children's Privacy

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn we have, we will delete it right away.

8. Security

We use reasonable protections like SSL encryption to keep your information safe. That said, no online method is ever 100% secure.

9. Other Websites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so check their policies too.

10. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time, and we will post any changes here.
