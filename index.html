Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your details and connect you with information on plans available for your address and selected service — real options based on what's offered near you.

Are you an internet provider?

No. Internet Cable N TV is operated by Zaz International Inc, an independent reseller. We are not affiliated with any specific provider — your chosen provider handles installation, billing, and support.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. If you're interested in more than one service — like internet and TV — just note it in your message and we'll include it in your request.

Is the ZIP code required?

Yes. Your ZIP code helps us confirm which plans and providers are actually available at your address.

Can I opt out after submitting?

Yes. You can opt out of calls, texts, or emails at any time by replying STOP or contacting us directly — opting out won't affect any existing service with a provider.
