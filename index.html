FAQ

Straight answers before you call

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your details and follow up with plan options available for your address and selected service.

Are you an internet provider?

No. Internet Cable N TV is operated by Zaz International Inc, an independent reseller. Your chosen provider handles installation, billing, and support.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. Note internet and TV (or other combos) in your message and we’ll include them in the request.

Is the ZIP code required?

Yes. ZIP codes help confirm which plans and providers are actually available at your address.

Can I opt out after submitting?

Yes. Reply STOP to texts or contact us anytime. Opting out won’t affect existing service with a provider.
